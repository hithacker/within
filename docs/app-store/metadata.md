# Within App Store Metadata

## Name

Within

## Subtitle

Notice your patterns earlier

## Promotional text

Write about your day, notice recurring patterns, and practice a useful life skill
when it matters.

## Description

Within is a private journal for adults who want to understand how everyday choices
become recurring patterns.

Write about your day in your own words. When you choose AI analysis, Within looks
across separate entries for repeated behavior, shows the evidence behind a
possible pattern, and offers one practical skill to try. There is no AI chat.

Features:

- Private daily journal entries stored on your device
- Optional, structured AI pattern analysis
- Evidence links back to the entries behind each pattern
- Practical skills for pacing, boundaries, balance, and conflict
- Controls to dismiss inaccurate observations
- Clear crisis and emergency support boundaries

Within uses artificial intelligence and can make mistakes. It does not diagnose
people, predict relationship outcomes, or decide what you should do. It is not
therapy, medical advice, or an emergency service. If you may be in immediate
danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted person.

## Review notes

Within is an AI-assisted journal and life-skills product, not a medical or
diagnostic app. AI processing is optional and disclosed before use. Outputs are
structured pattern candidates tied to source entries; there is no conversational
AI interface. Explicit self-harm, violence, abuse, and medical-emergency language
is deterministically routed to human support. No account is required in the
prototype.
